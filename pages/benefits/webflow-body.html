Understand what care may already be available to you

Many families are surprised to learn that their extended health benefits, HSA, or employer-sponsored coverage can help offset the cost of postpartum and newborn support. This guided tool helps you explore potential eligibility for services like:

  • Certified postpartum doula care
  • Private duty nursing
  • Registered massage therapy
  • Lactation support
  • Mental health support

And if benefits coverage isn't available, many families choose to add Alma Care gift cards to their baby registry — making meaningful postpartum support part of the care ecosystem from day one.

Personalized postpartum support, thoughtfully coordinated

Alma Care connects families with trusted postpartum professionals — including certified doulas, nurses, lactation consultants, and recovery specialists — to support recovery, newborn care, feeding, sleep, and the transition into early parenthood. Care is personalized to your family's needs and can often be coordinated alongside eligible insurance or HSA coverage pathways.

This tool is intended for educational and planning purposes only and is not a pre-determination of insurance coverage or eligibility. Coverage varies by provider and individual plan. We recommend confirming all details directly with your insurance provider or benefits administrator.

Where to find your coverage details

Look in your benefits booklet (often a PDF in your employer portal or available on your insurer's website). Look for sections labeled "Extended Health Care", "Paramedical Services", or "Health Spending Account". You'll find annual maximums and per-visit limits there.
