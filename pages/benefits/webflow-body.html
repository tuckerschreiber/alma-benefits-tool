Understand what care may already be available to you

Many families are surprised to learn that their extended health benefits, HSA, or employer-sponsored coverage can help offset the cost of postpartum and newborn support. This guided tool helps you explore potential eligibility for:

  • Postpartum doula care
  • In-home overnight support
  • Registered massage therapy
  • Lactation support
  • Mental health support

No coverage? Many families add Alma Care gift cards to their baby registry — friends and family can contribute directly to postpartum support.

For educational and planning purposes only — not a pre-determination of insurance coverage. Coverage varies by provider and plan; confirm details directly with your insurer.

Where to find your coverage details

Look in your benefits booklet (often a PDF in your employer portal or available on your insurer's website). Look for sections labeled "Extended Health Care", "Paramedical Services", or "Health Spending Account". You'll find annual maximums and per-visit limits there.
